## Maintaining the record

When maintaining Plotter, append useful feedback with the study/revision, date, user's preference, and any interpretation clearly distinguished. Preserve contradictory or superseded entries as evidence instead of quietly rewriting them into approval. Summarize a durable rule in its current authoritative document only when the feedback supports that scope.

A liked illustration, catalogue addition, or successful rendering does not establish a new reference selection or a reliable skill. Record what was actually inspected. Future evaluations can use the packaged workflows and examples; this reorganization does not claim a new clean-install or behavioral evaluation.
