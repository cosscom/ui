## Drawing process

The revision pass proceeded by subject group after the three liked examples established the direction. Each new drawing was compared at native 560 × 440 against a relevant existing reference. The comparisons considered main-form proportions, the relationship carrying the idea, useful guides, and label clearance. Refinements included shared-edge ownership, actual tangent connections, corrected clipping for negative space, measured intervals, and clearer labels. The integrated React drawings were also rendered together to inspect their appearance after conversion.

The initial collection contained eight agent concepts and five subjects each in geometry, mechanisms, objects, architecture, nature, music, maps, typography, interfaces, software, time, absence, craft, and play. Conceptual metaphors are identified in source metadata; dimensions describe the drawn geometry rather than asserting physical performance. Background grids were omitted where local geometry already explained the relationship.

This is an authored illustration revision, not a clean-install skill evaluation. No test suites, type checks, or automated audit campaigns were run, following the user’s request. Rendering and visual comparison informed the drawings; they do not establish engineering accuracy or broad skill reliability.
